How to play

Connect unrelated words via neighbors

Every day there are 5 word pairs, each consisting of a start and target word.

Click a word to view its neighbors

Neighbors are words that frequently appear in similar contexts and convey related meanings.

Example
whisper ⟶ shouting ⟶ scuffle
whisper and shouting are related by volume.
shouting connects to scuffle in the context of conflict.

Click neighbors that relate to the target word to reach it. The progress prompt bar shows how close you are to the target word.

A perfect solve is 2 jumps per pair, or 10 total for the day. Built with word2vec and Google Books.
