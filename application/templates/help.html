How to play

Connect two unrelated words by their neighbors.

Each day, there are 5 pairs of words. Each pair includes a start word, where you start from, and a target word, where you want to get to.

Click a word to view its neighbors.

These neighbors will always be similar in some way, but they are not obvious synonyms. Instead, they paint a picture of where a word appears: each neighbor shares a common context with the current word.

Clicking neighbors which also relate to the target word will help you navigate closer to your goal. The progress bar below the grid shows how close you are to the target.

Example
whisper ⟶ shouting ⟶ scuffle
whisper and shouting are related by volume.
shouting connects to scuffle in the context of conflict.

A perfect solve is 2 jumps per pair, or 10 total for the day. Built with word2vec and Google Books.
